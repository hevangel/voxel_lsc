# AGENTS.md — Maintaining the La Salle College 3D Campus

Guidance for AI agents (and humans) modifying this project. Read this before changing code.

## What this is

A self-contained, dependency-free (Three.js is vendored) 3D model of **La Salle College,
18 La Salle Road, Kowloon Tong, Hong Kong** (喇沙書院, founded 1932, motto *Fides et Opera*).
The user flies or walks freely, or runs an **auto flyover** that replays the camera path of one
of the two reference videos while that video plays picture-in-picture (bottom right).
Front page: `index.html`.

Fan-made tribute — **not affiliated with the school**. Keep that disclaimer in the UI.

## Ground truth

Build from the evidence in `reference/`, in this order of trust:
1. `reference/video/*.mp4` (the two flyover videos; gitignored, local only) and their frames.
2. `reference/photos/satellite_lsc_z19.jpg` — 3.62 px/m. The capture is oblique: roofs ~33 m up
   appear ~11 m north of their footprint. Measure footprints at the base, not the roof.
3. Ground photos in `reference/photos/`.
4. `reference/articles/RESEARCH_NOTES.md` (rev 3) — the verified layout, in world coordinates,
   with every claim tagged [V] verified / [M] measured / [?] unverified. Keep it current when
   you learn something new; check [?] items before relying on them.

## Running

- `node tools/dev-server.js .` → http://127.0.0.1:8137 (no-store caching, localhost only).
  Videos need HTTP serving for the PiP to play reliably; opening `index.html` from disk works
  otherwise (classic scripts, no ES modules).

### Coordinates and angles (critical)

- Metres. `+x` = east, `+z` = **south**, `+y` = up, `y 0` = track level (HKPD 14 m).
- The site is NOT flat (LandsD DTM): the track is a sunken terrace; the ring, pool and garden
  stand on the La Salle Road terrace at y 8.5 (lower quad 8.5, court 12), east courts y 10,
  ground north of the pool y ≈ 19.5. Outside the wall everything sits on `LSC.Terrain.raw`;
  use `LSC.Terrain.ground(x, z)` for open-ground height, never assume 0.
- Satellite grid → world: `x = gx − 130`, `z = 260 − gy`.
- Main ring x −66..18, z −57..4, roof y 33, with pinwheel corner arms (NW, NE, SE, SW —
  all classroom wing ends, no towers; crest on the SW arm's south end); void x −49..2, z −43..−12: lower quad (y 8.5) W of the retaining wall at x −19,
  basketball court a half level up (y 10.3) E of it — no block between them. Pool N of ring x −72..−22, z −80.5..−61.5.
  Track centre (2, 55.85). Exact numbers live at the top of `campus.js` / `context.js`.
- Camera angles everywhere: `yaw` = compass heading deg (0 = N = −z, 90 = E), `pitch` = deg above
  horizon, `roll` deg (**+ banks left**, horizon rises on the right — verified by render).

### Flyover paths

`LSC.PATHS.X = { title, video, duration, vfov, shots: [{ t0, t1, keys: [[t,x,y,z,yaw,pitch,roll,vfov?]…] }] }`.
Shots split only at real cuts/dissolves and must cover 0..duration contiguously; keys inside a
shot are Catmull-Rom. The flyover reads `video.currentTime`, so pause/seek move the camera; if
the video is missing it falls back to a timer.

**Calibrating:** `node tools/calib.js --path A --range 0:30:3 --out <scratch>/x.jpg` renders
[video frame | model] pairs (`--overlay` blends, `--poses file.json` renders free poses). Needs
ffmpeg and the Playwright copy bundled with `@playwright/cli`. Judge each cell strictly (same
landmark, same place in frame, similar size); never leave a key inside solid geometry.

## Conventions

- No build step, no framework, no ES modules; `node --check js/*.js` is the CI.
- All state under `window.LSC`; `LSC.App` is the wiring hub.
- Geometry goes through `LSC.Geo` material buckets (few draw calls); register colliders for
  anything the player can hit.
- Comments only where the code can't speak (why-coordinates, measurements, gotchas).

## Testing checklist (manual, ~3 min)

1. Load → bar completes → landing over a slow orbit.
2. Fly freely: WASD, Space/C up/down, Shift fast, walls block. F → walk (gravity, jump).
3. Auto flyover A and B: video plays bottom-right, camera follows it; pause/seek/mute work;
   Esc or a move key hands over free flight at the current pose; end of video hands over too.
4. ⌂ returns to the landing screen.
